04 — Story

Why JITT exists

JITT started in a half-empty gym with borrowed plates and a group chat. No sponsors, no shortcuts — just a handful of athletes who wanted programming that respected their time and kit that didn't fall apart by week three.

We still make everything the same way: test it ourselves, keep what works, bin what doesn't. If it isn't good enough for our own squad, it doesn't get our name on it.

— The JITT Athletics team
